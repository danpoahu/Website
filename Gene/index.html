In loving memory

Eugene Paul Pellegrini

“Gene”

May 18, 1937 – October 1, 2026

✦

Eugene (Gene) Paul Pellegrini, a resident of Santa Rosa, California, passed away on October 1, 2026, at the age of 90.

Gene was born on May 18, 1937, in Healdsburg at the “old hospital.” He was raised in Cloverdale and attended school there. He was a 1955 graduate of Cloverdale High School.

Gene received a BA in Liberal Arts from San Francisco State University in 1960. The class was so large that the ceremony was held at the Cow Palace. He also served in the U.S. Navy Reserve for six years, reporting to the Alameda Naval Air Base. His training was in Millington, TN.

After graduation, Gene worked at the Chevron Headquarters at 225 Bush St. in San Francisco for 12 years. During this time in the city, he met his partner of 52 years, the late Lee Morris. Gene loved every minute of living in the city, and driving his Chevy Malibu convertible around the Bay Area.

Later, Gene and Lee owned and operated a Prudential Real Estate franchise in San Anselmo for 20 years. They resided in Fairfax until 2015, when they moved to the Fountaingrove Lodge in Santa Rosa to be closer to family. They traveled locally and also vacationed in Palm Springs.

Gene was an avid art collector, with a special interest in California artists. He enjoyed dining out, fine cuisine, and the theater. North Beach was a favorite spot. He kept up with city news through daily readings of the Chronicle.

He leaves behind a beloved family of six nieces and nephews, their spouses, many grandnieces and nephews, a sister-in-law, and friends at Fountaingrove Lodge.

Gene was predeceased by his parents, Ruth Finney Pellegrini and Raoul Pellegrini, sister Fay Seghesio, brother-in-law Ed Seghesio, and brother Richard Pellegrini. Gene and Lee were together until Lee’s passing in 2023.

The family wishes to thank the Fountaingrove Lodge staff for their care and support.

A private family service will be held. Arrangements by The Neptune Society.
