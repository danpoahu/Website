Rudolph's story

For fifteen years, Rudolph has been Christopher's constant companion. He's a sweet, sun-loving senior dog, and now he is nearing the end of his life.

Christopher wants to give Rudolph a calm, painless goodbye with a veterinarian, and to bring his ashes home so he can stay close. Money is tight right now, though, and he can't cover euthanasia and cremation by himself.

That's why he started a small fundraiser. Anyone who has said goodbye to a dog knows how much it matters to do it with dignity, without worrying about the bill.
