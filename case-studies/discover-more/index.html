Discover More Help
I closed the app. Did I lose my answers?
No worries, your answers are saved! Open Discover More again and tap Take Surveys to pick up where you left off. We also emailed you a link back.
How do I see my results again?
Tap Survey Results on your dashboard, or use the link in your results email. You can download a PDF too.
